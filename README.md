## ⚡ 3 Signature Interactive Experiences

1. **The Builder's Log & Project Radar (`web/ecosystem-hub.html#radar`)**
   - Live telemetry across 60 active projects (RingSizer, MirrorMate, Reviewell, Qdocs, HarborPulse).
   - Details: *What was built*, *The unexpected bottleneck*, *The practical lesson*, and *Verified metrics*.
   - Filterable across Shipped Apps, AI & Reputation, Commerce OS, and Maritime Logistics.

2. **The AI & Automation Discovery Lab (`web/ecosystem-hub.html#ai-lab`)**
   - Verified workflows without black-box marketing claims.
   - Evaluates: Guardrail Boundaries, Observed Latencies, Cost Uncertainty, and Human-in-the-Loop requirements.
   - Includes real code snippets for ElevenLabs synthesis, Policy 7400114 legal escalation, and DuckDB analytics.

3. **60-Second Operational & Reputation Diagnostician (`web/ecosystem-hub.html#diagnostician`)**
   - Dual-track diagnostic calculator:
     - **Track A (Commerce & Spreadsheets):** Evaluates formula breakage risk across multi-currency stores.
     - **Track B (Reputation & Google Reviews):** Evaluates Policy 7400114 eligibility and generative search (Perplexity/ChatGPT) citation disqualification.
   - Outputs an instant risk score and 3-step prioritized remediation plan with zero login barrier.
